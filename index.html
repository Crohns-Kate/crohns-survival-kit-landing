How NeuroGut Acoustics™ Helps

A wellbeing and self-management platform designed to give you insight and awareness—not diagnosis or treatment.

♪

GI Sound Check

Use your smartphone to capture short recordings of gut sounds by placing it gently on your abdomen—completely non-invasive.

≈

Pattern Insights

AI-assisted analysis highlights general activity patterns—like quieter periods, more active times, or clustered sounds. Non-diagnostic awareness.

⟲

Gut–Brain Awareness

Log stress levels, sleep quality, meals, and mood to see how they might relate to your gut activity over time.

↗

Better Conversations

Export a simple summary of your patterns to share with family or bring to clinician appointments for more informed discussions.

Important: NeuroGut Acoustics™ provides insight and awareness to support your wellbeing journey—it does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical care.

About the Founder

Dr. Michael Bishopp

NeuroGut Acoustics™ is led by Dr. Michael Bishopp, a Gold Coast–based health founder with over 15 years of experience in patient-facing roles and building clinic systems.

He combines lived experience of complex gut challenges with a deep interest in nervous-system regulation, digital health, and AI-enabled tools. His approach focuses on creating practical, compassionate solutions that empower people to better understand their bodies and engage more confidently with their care teams.
